## Part B: double blink without Fp1

PhysionetMI, 50 subjects, 300 imagery runs, 617 minutes of signal, 5 min. Reference: the detector on Fp1 (default thresholds) finds 6407 blinks (10.4 per minute), 6101 of them strong (≥ 100 µV, like a deliberate blink), and 479 double blinks.

Blink amplitude on the mean of FC3 and FC4, relative to Fp1 (0.1-8 Hz): median 0.32, quartiles 0.26-0.41.

Detector on FC3/FC4: signal (`mean` of the two channels, or `min`, which needs both to rise as a symmetric blink does), adaptive factor k (threshold = the larger of the minimum amplitude and k times the robust background deviation; Fp1 uses k = 5, minimum 60 µV). A detection matches a reference blink within 150 ms. False detections are blinks found on FC3/FC4 with no Fp1 blink nearby; double blinks found on FC3/FC4 with no Fp1 double blink nearby are false `action` commands.
